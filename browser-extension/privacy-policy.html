🔐 Privacy Policy & Permissions

Transparency and control over your data

Last Updated: January 2025
👋 Introduction

At Ovara, we believe in complete transparency about what data we access, why we need it, and how we use it. This policy explains every permission the Ovara Assistant requests and gives you full control over which features to enable.

🛡️ Our Core Principles:
  • No data selling - We never sell your data to third parties
  • Minimal collection - We only collect what's necessary for features
  • Your control - Every permission is optional and can be revoked
  • Local first - Most data stays on your device
  • Encryption - Data in transit is encrypted
🔑 Permissions Explained

Below are all permissions the Ovara Assistant may request. Each permission enables specific features.

🌐 Active Tab Access Required
Access to read the content of the webpage you're currently viewing.
Why we need this: To provide page-aware assistance, detect page type, extract content for summarization, and understand context for relevant suggestions.

What we access: Page title, URL, visible text content, and page structure.

What we DON'T access: Passwords, payment information, or data from other tabs.
💾 Local Storage Required
Store your settings, preferences, and session data locally on your device.
Why we need this: Save your theme preferences, position settings, usage statistics, and authentication tokens.

What we store: Settings, user tier, request counts, and login sessions.

Location: All data stored locally in Chrome's secure storage (not on our servers).
📋 Clipboard Access Optional
Read text you copy to provide instant assistance with copied content.
Why we need this: Enable "Clipboard Monitor" feature - shows notification when you copy text and offers instant actions (rewrite, summarize, translate).

What we access: Only text content from your clipboard, only when Ovara Assistant is active.

Control: Can be disabled in Settings → Privacy → Clipboard Monitor.
⌨️ Text Input Monitoring Optional
Monitor what you type to provide auto-suggestions and writing assistance.
Why we need this: Enable "Auto-Suggestions" feature - provides real-time writing suggestions as you type, like GitHub Copilot for writing.

What we access: Text in active input fields (only when you're typing and assistant is enabled).

Privacy safeguards:
  • Never monitors password fields
  • Never monitors payment/credit card fields
  • 500ms delay after you stop typing before analyzing
  • Only last sentence sent for suggestions
Control: Can be disabled in Settings → Privacy → Auto-Suggestions.
📧 Email Content Access (Gmail/Outlook) Optional
Read email threads to provide context-aware email assistance.
Why we need this: Enable "Email Thread Analyzer" - summarizes long email chains and helps draft context-aware responses.

What we access: Email content in active thread only, when you click "Analyze Thread" button.

What we DON'T access: Other emails, contacts, or email metadata without explicit action.

Control: Can be disabled in Settings → Privacy → Email Analysis.
🎤 Microphone Access Optional
Use voice input for hands-free prompts and dictation.
Why we need this: Enable "Voice Input" feature - speak your prompts instead of typing them.

What we access: Audio from microphone only when you click the microphone button.

Privacy: Uses browser's built-in Web Speech API - no audio sent to Ovara servers, processed locally by browser.

Control: Browser will prompt for permission each time, can be disabled in Settings → Privacy → Voice Input.
🖱️ Context Menu (Right-Click) Optional
Add "Ask Ovara" option to right-click menu on selected text.
Why we need this: Enable quick access - right-click any text → "Ask Ovara" → instant assistance.

What we access: Only text you explicitly right-click on.

Control: Can be disabled in Settings → Privacy → Context Menu.
🎥 Meeting Tab Detection Optional
Detect when you're in Google Meet or Zoom to offer meeting assistance.
Why we need this: Enable "Meeting Notes Assistant" - automatically offers to take notes and extract action items from meetings.

What we access: Only tab URL to detect meeting platforms (meet.google.com, zoom.us). We do NOT access meeting audio or video.

Control: Can be disabled in Settings → Privacy → Meeting Assistant.

🔒 Data Security: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3. We use Supabase for authentication and database, which is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.
🚫 What We DON'T Do
  • We DON'T sell your data - Ever. To anyone. For any reason.
  • We DON'T track you across websites - No analytics, no fingerprinting, no tracking pixels.
  • We DON'T access password fields - Automatically excluded from all monitoring.
  • We DON'T access payment information - Credit card fields are blacklisted.
  • We DON'T read your emails - Unless you explicitly click "Analyze Thread".
  • We DON'T record audio - Voice input uses browser API, no recording.
  • We DON'T store page content - Content sent to AI is not permanently stored.
  • We DON'T share with third parties - Except OpenAI for AI processing (see below).
🤝 Third-Party Services

Ovara uses the following third-party services:

🤖 OpenAI (ChatGPT API)
Powers the AI assistance features.
What we share: Your prompts and context (selected text, page content when requested).

Their policy: OpenAI stores prompts for 30 days for abuse prevention, then deletes. They do not train models on API data.

Privacy: OpenAI Privacy Policy
🔐 Supabase
Authentication and database infrastructure.
What we share: Your email, authentication tokens, usage statistics.

Security: SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, data encrypted at rest and in transit.

Privacy: Supabase Privacy Policy
⚖️ Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access - Request a copy of all data we have about you
  • Right to Deletion - Request permanent deletion of your account and data
  • Right to Correction - Update or correct your information
  • Right to Portability - Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Opt-Out - Disable any optional features at any time
  • Right to Object - Object to data processing for specific purposes
📧 Exercise Your Rights: Contact us at privacy@ovara.app to exercise any of these rights. We'll respond within 30 days.
⚙️ Managing Your Permissions

You have full control over which features to enable:

In Extension Settings:

  1. Click the Ovara extension icon
  2. Click "Settings"
  3. Go to "Privacy & Permissions" tab
  4. Toggle individual features on/off

In Browser Settings:

  1. Chrome → Settings → Privacy and Security → Site Settings
  2. Find Ovara extension
  3. Revoke specific permissions (clipboard, microphone, etc.)
💡 Tip: Disabling a permission will disable related features but won't affect other functionality. You can re-enable at any time.
📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as we add new features or improve existing ones. When we make changes:

  • We'll notify you via email and in-app notification
  • We'll highlight what changed
  • You'll have the option to review and accept changes
  • If you don't accept, affected features will be disabled

You can always view the current version at this page.
